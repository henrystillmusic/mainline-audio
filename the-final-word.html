Character

Five modes. One ceiling.

Each character mode applies a distinct algorithm inside the oversampled limiting stage. Mix it in parallel — the ceiling is always enforced on the blend output.

Clean
Pure transparent brickwall limiting with no additional harmonic processing.
Soft
Softens the transition into limiting for a gentler, less abrupt presentation while preserving the source material.
Warm
Introduces tube-inspired warmth and subtle harmonic enrichment that increases naturally with drive.
Punchy
Preserves transient energy while adding presence and impact where the material demands it.
Vintage
Introduces tape-inspired high-frequency softening and even-harmonic saturation that increases with drive.
Monitoring

Four ways to hear the truth

Every monitoring tool is latency-compensated. Bypass, Gain Match, Delta, and A/B give you a complete and honest read of what the limiter is doing.

Bypass
Latency-compensated pass-through of the pre-trim source. True A/B against the unprocessed signal — Input Gain not applied. 512-sample equal-power crossfade, click-free.
Gain Match
All processing active, output RMS-matched to the pre-trim source. Removes the loudness bias from comparisons so you hear the character of the limiting, not its level.
Delta
Outputs the difference between the dry and processed signal. Silence means the limiter isn't acting. A true null test for limiting and character.
A / B
Two fully independent parameter snapshots. Switch between settings instantly with a click-free handoff — no artifacts at the crossover point.
Metering

Read what matters

No blurred distinctions — sample peaks and true peaks are shown separately, and every meter tells you exactly what it's measuring.

Output L/R
Sample-peak level bars in dBFS from the final output. Switches to Mid and Side in M/S mode. 2-second peak hold with clip indicator.
True Peak
Oversampled inter-sample peak in dBTP. Ceiling enforcement uses a dedicated 8× stage independent of the main Oversampling setting. 2-second hold.
GR + Max GR
Gain reduction bar (0 to −20 dB) and session-maximum numeric readout. Max GR captures peaks beyond the bar's display range.
Integrated LUFS
Overall loudness across the full track, measured to broadcast standard.
Short-Term LUFS
3-second sliding window K-weighted loudness per EBU R128.
PSR
Peak-to-Short-Term Loudness Ratio — True Peak minus Short-Term LUFS. Real-time dynamic headroom indicator.
LRA
How much the loudness varies across the track — from quiet passages to loud ones.
In Delta mode, LUFS, PSR, LRA, and the waveform display all reflect the residual signal — what limiting and character are adding or removing.
