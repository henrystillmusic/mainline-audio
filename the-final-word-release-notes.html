What changed in each version of the mastering limiter, and what it means when you are working.

September 2026

1.1.0

Released 8 September 2026

A focused update to true-peak handling, monitoring and day-to-day workflow.

  • True Peak. Refined final-output true-peak protection and metering. With True Peak enabled, the Ceiling is enforced against reconstructed true peaks; with it disabled, the Ceiling applies to sample peaks, while the true-peak meters continue to show reconstructed output peaks either way.
  • Auto Release. Improved adaptive release behavior on dense and sustained material. Auto Release continues to work across a 100–600 ms range.
  • Gain Match. Corrected level-matching for more reliable comparisons between the source and the fully processed result.
  • Bypass. Improved transition behavior for cleaner switching in and out of processing.
  • Metering & interface. Refined meter scaling and spacing, clearer Ceiling reference presentation, and general typography and sizing polish.
  • Tooltips & User Guide. Expanded plain-language guidance throughout the interface, with direct access to the new User Guide from the About panel.
  • Maintenance. Additional stability and workflow refinements.

Existing owners can download 1.1.0 from the original Lemon Squeezy download link in their purchase email.
