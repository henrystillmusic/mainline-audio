Motion

Three macros. One evolving shape.

Focus and Spread set where the eight stages sit relative to each other. Drift adds a slow, continuous wobble modeled on tape and vinyl movement.

Focus
The center frequency every stage's own position is measured from — move it and the whole formation follows.
Spread
Sets how far the eight stages fan out from Focus, from tightly clustered to widely spaced.
Drift
A slow, continuous pitch/time wobble in Tape or Vinyl character, from barely-there to obviously alive.
Routing

Shape the relationship between stages

Beyond static filtering, two independent motion generators and per-stage mix control let the eight-stage formation move and blend in real time.

Motion
The first of two independent motion generators, modulating stage frequency for continuous, musical movement.
Motion 2
A second, independent motion generator — layer it against Motion for more complex, less repetitive movement.
Stage Mix
Blend each stage's processed signal back against dry, independently, for exact control over how present each stage's effect is.
Independent Sidechain
Drive Motion from an external or internal sidechain input alongside Motion's own free-running generators, for movement that responds to the material itself.
Sync & Offset

Lock it to the groove, or don't

Motion and Drift can run free or lock to the host tempo, with per-stage phase offset for movement that unfolds across the eight stages rather than all at once.

Tempo Sync
Motion and Motion 2 can each free-run or lock to host tempo, independently, per stage or globally.
Phase Offset
Offsets each stage's own motion phase, so movement can cascade across the eight stages instead of moving in lockstep.
Global Motion Intensity
One macro that scales the combined depth of every stage's motion at once, for a fast overall push without touching individual stages.
