Privacy Policy

mainline-audio.com · Updated November 21, 2026

Overview

Mainline Audio LLC ("Mainline Audio," "we," "us") operates mainline-audio.com, including the Mainline Audio DSP product pages. This policy explains what information is collected on this site, how we use it, and where a third-party service is responsible instead of us.

Information You Provide Directly

If you use a contact or mailing-list form on this site, you provide your name, email address, and any optional details you choose to add (for example, your DAW or a link to a track). Forms on this site are delivered through Formspree, a third-party form-delivery service, directly to Mainline Audio's inbox. We use this information to respond to you and, if you signed up for updates, to follow up about the product or updates you signed up for. If you'd rather not be contacted again, email support@mainline-audio.com and we'll remove your information from our records.

Purchases and Demo Downloads

The Last Look, The Final Word, and The Fine Line are sold by Mainline Audio LLC through Stripe Checkout. Stripe collects your payment details and the contact information needed for the transaction. We receive your checkout email and relevant order information, including the product, checkout reference, and payment status, to fulfill your order, issue your license, and provide support. Mainline Audio does not receive or store your full payment card details. We use Resend to deliver the license email, which includes your license key.

You can download the installers for The Last Look, The Final Word, and The Fine Line directly from their product pages. Starting the 14-day demo in the software does not require checkout, payment, or an email address.

For purchases or free demo checkouts still offered through Lemon Squeezy, that service collects the information needed to process the order and deliver access. It remains the merchant of record for purchases it processed, including earlier The Last Look orders. See Lemon Squeezy's Privacy Policy and Buyer Terms.

License Activation & Validation

The Last Look, The Final Word, and The Fine Line use Mainline Audio's licensing service for Mainline keys, hosted on Cloudflare. During activation, the software sends your license key and a computer or instance name derived from your Mac's computer name. During validation or deactivation, it sends your license key and the activation instance identifier. The service uses this information to verify the license and manage its activations. These requests do not include your email address. The service may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address.

The licensing service keeps records associated with your license, such as the product, checkout email and reference, a hashed license key, and activation records. Mainline Audio uses them for fulfillment, validation, and support. The software can also keep demo and license information locally on your Mac, including credentials in the macOS Keychain where supported.

Lemon Squeezy keys that remain supported, including existing keys for The Final Word and The Fine Line, continue to use the Lemon Squeezy licensing service for the same purposes. Products with an update checker request a small version file from mainline-audio.com. The update request does not deliberately include your license key or activation identifiers.

Information Collected Automatically

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most hosting providers, GitHub may log standard technical information (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and operational purposes, described in GitHub's own Privacy Statement. Mainline Audio does not separately access or analyze this data.

This site does not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or other behavioral-tracking scripts, and does not set its own cookies. Some pages use your browser's local storage to remember background-music playback settings (like volume and which track was playing) so the audio player can resume between visits — this information stays on your own device and is never sent to Mainline Audio.

Do Not Track & Browser Privacy Signals

This site does not currently change its behavior in response to a browser's Do Not Track setting or a Global Privacy Control signal. There's no first-party tracking here to turn off in the first place — Mainline Audio doesn't run its own advertising or behavioral-tracking technology on this site. Third-party services or embedded content (see below) may independently collect information according to their own privacy practices, which this site doesn't control and isn't able to override.

Third-Party Content on This Site

  • The Music page embeds Spotify players. Loading one connects your browser directly to Spotify, under Spotify's Privacy Policy.
  • Some pages load the Satoshi typeface from Fontshare, a third-party font-hosting service.
  • Some pages load a waveform-display script from a public code CDN.

These are independent services; their own privacy practices, not this policy, govern what they collect.

Service Providers, Selling & Advertising

We use service providers to operate the site, deliver form submissions, process purchases, manage licenses, and send transactional emails. The providers involved depend on the product and checkout you use, as described above.

Mainline Audio does not sell personal information or share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral or targeted advertising.

Retention

We retain correspondence, order information, and licensing records as reasonably necessary to respond to you, support your order or license, and keep business records. We delete messages submitted through our forms on request. Payment and other service providers may keep information under their own policies.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information sent to us, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we can't guarantee it.

Your Choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, request a correction or deletion, or opt out of follow-up emails by contacting support@mainline-audio.com. For information a checkout provider handles directly, see the privacy policy of Stripe or Lemon Squeezy linked above.

California Residents

If you're a California resident, you may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) regarding personal information collected about you, depending on whether Mainline Audio meets the Act's applicability thresholds. As stated above, Mainline Audio does not sell personal information and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To make a privacy request, email support@mainline-audio.com.

International Users

Mainline Audio does not maintain an EU-based representative or Data Protection Officer. If you're located outside the United States and choose to submit information to us directly, understand it will be processed in the United States. Stripe and Lemon Squeezy describe their international data-transfer practices in their respective privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

This site and Mainline Audio DSP products are aimed at professional and hobbyist audio engineers, not children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, email support@mainline-audio.com and we'll delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the site or our products change. The effective date above reflects the most recent update.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@mainline-audio.com.
