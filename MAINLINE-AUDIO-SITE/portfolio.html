Mission Statement

My goal is to build a sustainable career as an audio mastering engineer while continuing to create and release my own music. I focus on delivering high-quality masters that translate across every listening environment — from club systems to earbuds — so artists can present their work at its highest level without distraction.

Through Mainline Audio LLC, I combine 30+ years of hands-on music experience with professional-grade technical training to serve independent artists across electronic, hip-hop, indie, and experimental genres. My approach is rooted in clarity, consistency, and trust — working toward long-term creative partnerships rather than one-off transactions.
