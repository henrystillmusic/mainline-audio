Mastering Workflow

Process

Mastering is the final stage of audio production where technical precision and critical listening converge to prepare music for translation across modern playback systems and distribution formats.

Routine Procedures

  • Optimize average and peak volume levels from track to track
  • Create tonal balance and consistency across an entire project
  • Select appropriate analog and/or digital processing for the submitted material
  • Apply equalization, compression, limiting, and other dynamics processing as necessary
  • Adjust spacing, fades, and transitions between tracks
  • Edit minor clicks, pops, and unwanted noise
  • Apply restoration or noise reduction processes when required
  • Sequence tracks into final album or EP format
  • Insert PQ subcodes and track IDs for CD replication
  • Embed ISRC codes and CD-Text metadata as needed
  • Prepare final deliverables including WAV, DDP, vinyl pre-masters, and streaming-ready formats
  • Perform quality control checks on all final masters and deliverables
  • Archive project files and maintain organized backup systems

Quality Control

Every master is evaluated for tonal balance, stereo imaging, dynamics, translation, metadata integrity, and technical compliance across delivery formats.

Delivery Formats

WAV DDP Vinyl Pre-Masters Streaming Masters Instrumentals Alternate Versions

Monitoring

Monitoring decisions are referenced across multiple playback environments to support consistency, translation, and long-term listening confidence.
