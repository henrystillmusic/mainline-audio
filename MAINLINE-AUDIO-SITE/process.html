Mastering Workflow

Process

Every mix that arrives here gets the same treatment: careful listening, deliberate processing, and honest perspective built on decades of hearing music everywhere from club systems to earbuds. The goal is always translation - your music sounding the way you intended it, on everything it plays on.

The Work

  • Optimize average and peak levels across all tracks in a project
  • Shape tonal balance and consistency from track to track
  • Apply precision digital processing - EQ, compression, limiting - suited to the material
  • Target loudness levels for streaming platforms and format-specific delivery
  • Adjust spacing, fades, and transitions between tracks
  • Edit minor clicks, pops, and digital artifacts
  • Apply restoration or noise reduction when required
  • Remaster from tape, CD, or legacy analog and digital sources when needed
  • Sequence tracks into final album or EP format
  • Embed ISRC codes and release metadata
  • Prepare final deliverables including WAV, DDP, vinyl pre-masters, and streaming-ready formats
  • Quality control across multiple playback systems before delivery
  • Archive project files on completion

Quality Control

Every master is evaluated for tonal balance, stereo imaging, dynamics, translation, metadata integrity, and technical compliance across delivery formats.

Delivery Formats

Monitoring

References span multiple playback environments - precision near-fields, consumer earbuds, and portable speakers - to ensure the master holds up wherever it lands.
