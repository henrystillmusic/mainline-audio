The Focal Point Release Notes

What changed in each version of the spectral motion processor, and what it means when you are working.

October 2026

1.0.2

Released 20 August 2026

A maintenance release covering the plugin's first in-app update check and a licensing reliability fix.

  • Update check. The Focal Point now checks once, shortly after you open it, for a newer stable version, with an UPDATE AVAILABLE badge in the header.
  • Licensing. Fixed a rare crash that could occur if the plugin's editor was closed while a license check was still completing in the background.

Parameters, presets, DSP processing and saved-session compatibility are unchanged. Existing projects open normally.

Existing owners can download 1.0.2 from the original Lemon Squeezy download link in their purchase email.
